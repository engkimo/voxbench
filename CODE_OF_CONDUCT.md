# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

VoxBench contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to a
harassment-free, welcoming, and technically constructive environment regardless
of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Assume good faith while asking for reproducible facts.
- Make room for newcomers and explain project-specific terminology.
- Respect privacy boundaries around call recordings, provider accounts, and
  operational data.
- Accept that maintainers may reject a technically interesting change when its
  evidence semantics, privacy, or realtime safety are unclear.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing call audio, transcripts, caller identifiers, credentials, or
  operational details without authorization.
- Pressuring people for reviews, access, endorsements, stars, or public support.
- Deliberately misrepresenting measurements, capture coverage, or test results.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project events, and public spaces when someone is representing VoxBench.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate public moderation need, use GitHub's report and block tools.
For a report requiring privacy, contact the maintainer through a private contact
method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private channel is
available, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact route and include no
sensitive details.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect the reporter's privacy as
far as practical, and may edit or remove content, warn participants, temporarily
limit participation, or permanently remove a participant from project spaces.
Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately where
appropriate, and free from retaliation.
